QUESTION — Outreach Email
You are preparing a cold-email outreach campaign targeting 10 potential clients. The purpose of the outreach is to get a response from the potential client.
From the actions below, select the three that you believe would have the greatest impact on increasing the likelihood that a potential client responds to your email.
Your answer will be considered correct if your top 3 is inside the top 4.

A. Keep the email under 100 words.
B. Use a spell checker to correct spelling and grammar before sending.
C. Before sending the outreach emails, identify which of the 10 potential clients have active email addresses that are regularly monitored.
D. Send the email from business@company.com rather than company@gmail.com.
E. Create and use a professional company email signature.
F. Avoid including attachments in the initial outreach email.
G. Send a customized email to each potential client.

Top 4:
A, B, D, E



QUESTION 2/6
You are responsible for setting up and managing social media for a startup that is preparing to launch a new chess web app similar to Chess.com.
The company currently has no established social media audience.
Goal: Within 1 month, acquire at least 1 paying subscriber/user through social media.
Budget: $0
Resources: You will be primarily responsible for managing the company's social media, so the amount of time available for creating content, posting, engaging with users, and growing accounts is 10 hours a week.
Which approach would you take to maximize the chance to achieve the goal.
( ) A. Focus on a single social media platform. First, you should research which platform is most suitable for what we want to achieve, then focus on that platform rather than diluting our limited resources across several platforms. Giving 20% effort to five platforms is less effective than giving 100% effort to one well-chosen platform.
( ) B. Try all social media platforms.

Ans: B


QUESTION 3/6

The objective is specifically to increase the number of paid members acquired through YouTube.
If you could choose only one option as the primary basis for your plan, which would you choose?

( ) A. Increase the number of videos posted per day.
( ) B. Analyze which existing videos are generating the most paid-member conversions, identify what those videos have in common, and use these findings to create and test more content.
( ) C. Focus on increasing the number of YouTube subscribers, since a larger subscriber count should eventually result in more paid members.
( ) D. Focus on increasing total video views by choosing topics with the greatest potential reach, regardless of whether those viewers have previously converted into paid members.

QUESTION 4/6

( ) A. Create a professional "Welcome to XLChess" post introducing the company, explaining what XLChess is, and briefly describing what the product offers.

QUESTION 6/6
XLChess is a startup developing a chess web application similar to Chess.com. The product will offer free features as well as a paid membership.
The company's immediate objective is to acquire its first 100 paid subscribers. The company believes that reaching the first 100 is the most difficult stage; once this has been achieved, it expects to have enough information and traction to make further growth significantly easier.
The company has a 3-month plan to reach this target:

Month 1 — Build and Prepare
Development Team (3 FTE)
Complete and launch the beta version.
Prioritise the core features that give users a reason to become paid members.
Growth & Marketing (1 FTE)
Establish and begin growing the company's social media presence across multiple platforms.
Start testing different types of content and approaches.

Month 2 — Membership Growth
The company's primary focus switches from building new features to acquiring paid members.
The Development Team will support Growth & Marketing where required—for example, by writing technical/development content that can be used on social media and helping produce material about the product.
The company will test approximately 20 different approaches to acquiring users. These may include:
Advertisement emails to smaller and mid-sized chess YouTube channels.
YouTube and social media content, targeting approximately one post/video per day.
Approaching chess clubs.
Approaching famous chess GMs
Sponsor online chess tournaments

Month 3 — Scale What Works
Review the results from Month 2.
Stop or reduce activities that have produced poor results and concentrate the company's resources on the approaches that have demonstrated the greatest potential.
Build on and expand the strategies that are successfully generating paid members.

Your budget is $100 for this 3 month plan and you can spend this money without requiring any approval.

Your Task
Spend as much time as you need to identify how you fit into this 3-month plan and write a timeline/task breakdown of what you would do.
The goal is for you to show us that you understand what is needed and that you know how to execute it.
Not much detail is required. A sentence giving an overview of what you intend to do is enough.
Treat this as a realistic exercise. Write the plan as if you had been hired for the Growth & Marketing role
